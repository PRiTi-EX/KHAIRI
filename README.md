# KHAIRI

> A tigress is missing. A forest station is silent.
> Somewhere inside the network, clues are waiting to be discovered.

KHAIRI is a story-driven, VulnHub-style standalone Linux CTF lab inspired by the forests of Similipal, Odisha.

**Find Khairi. Follow the trail. Reach the root.**

---

## 🐅 Story

Khairi is missing.

A silent forest station, forgotten records, hidden services, and scattered clues are waiting to be discovered.

Your investigation starts with a simple web server, but the deeper you go, the more secrets you uncover.

Can you follow the trail and find Khairi?

---

## 🎯 Objective

Investigate the KHAIRI machine, discover the hidden clues, gain access, escalate your privileges, and find the final flag.

---
